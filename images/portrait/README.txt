YOUR PORTRAIT PHOTOS
====================

Save your photo(s) here.

Suggested filename: sofia.jpg

Then in about.html, find the comment that says
"HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTO" and follow the instructions there.

Also in index.html, you can optionally update the "About Sofia"
teaser card to use your portrait instead of the landscape photo
(instructions are in the HTML comment in that file).

Recommended: portrait orientation (taller than wide), at least 800px wide.
