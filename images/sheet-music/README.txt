SHEET MUSIC COVER IMAGES
========================

Save your book/score cover images here.

Suggested filenames:
  cover-physical.jpg   — for the printed book
  cover-digital.jpg    — for the PDF/digital edition

Then in sheet-music.html, find the comment "HOW TO ADD COVER"
above each card and follow the instructions there.

Recommended: portrait orientation (like a book cover), at least 400px wide.
