ALBUM COVERS
============

Save your album cover images here.

For the "Sky" album:
  - Save the cover as: sky.jpg
  - The website will pick it up automatically.

For future albums:
  - Save the cover as: album-name.jpg (use lowercase, no spaces)
  - Then add a new card in music.html and a new entry in js/main.js
    (instructions are inside both files).

Recommended: square images, at least 600x600 pixels.
